A story about Scruge

The Same‑Toy Detective

How we find the exact same thing in other shops, explained for a five‑year‑old.

You found a toy you really like.

It is in a box from one shop called Amazon.

You wonder: do other shops have the exact same one?

Scruge is a detective that finds out for you.

2

First, we read the box very carefully.

We write down three things:

1. Who made it.
2. What it is called.
3. Its special thing.

These three things are our clues.

3

Every kind of toy has its own special thing.

It is the clue that tells two look-alike toys apart.

Phones

How much it can remember

TVs

How big the screen is

Shoes

What colour they are

T‑shirts

What colour they are

4

Then we visit the other shops.

For phones and TVs we go to Flipkart and Croma. For shoes and t‑shirts we go to Flipkart and Myntra.

At each shop we ask: "Do you have this one?"

We visit them all at the same time, so it's quick.

5

The shop shows us lots and lots of toys.

Many of them look the same. But most of them are not.

One is made by someone else. One is a bigger, newer version. One remembers less. One is a different colour.

6

So we play a game called "Is it the same?"

We check every toy, one clue at a time.

Every answer has to be YES. If even one answer is no, that toy is out.

Same maker?OnePlus and OnePlus
YES
Same name?Nord CE6 Lite and Nord CE6 Lite
YES
Same special thing?8 + 128 GB and 8 + 128 GB
YES

It's the same toy!

7

Almost the same is not the same.

A tiny difference makes it a different toy. So we are very, very picky.

The word "Plus" makes it a bigger phone.

Same shoe, different colour: not the same.

8

Clothes are the trickiest toys of all.

Lots of t‑shirts look almost the same. So we look for extra clues, best clue first.

1

The secret tag

Clothes have a code on their tag. If the codes match, it is the same shirt!

2

Look closely

Long sleeves or short? Collar or round neck? Plain or printed? Every clue must agree.

3

Just plain?

Two plain black t‑shirts can look the same. Then we say "maybe" and ask you to check.

9

Last question: can you take it home today?

In stock

The shop has it and you can buy it. We show you where.

Out of stock

The shop sells it, but the shelf is empty right now.

Not listed

None of this shop's toys passed the game. They don't sell it.

And if a shop's door is locked (the website doesn't answer), we say "couldn't check". We never pretend that means they don't sell it.

10

That's the whole trick!

Every time you give Scruge a link, it does these four things, in this order:

Read the box

2

3

4
